Kautilya Vidyalaya, Mysuru

Empowering young minds with value-based CBSE education, state-of-the-art facilities, and holistic growth.

About the Institution

Located in Kanakadasa Nagar, Dattagalli, Mysuru, Kautilya Vidyalaya is a premier CBSE-affiliated co-educational institution. Known for achieving academic excellence combined with top-tier athletic and creative infrastructure, the school provides an environment where students develop 21st-century skills, ethical grounding, and leadership.

From award-winning sustainability initiatives (Wipro Earthian Award) to advanced STEM and Atal Tinkering labs, Kautilya Vidyalaya stands as a beacon of modern progressive education in Mysuru.

Main Campus & Architecture

Spanning a thoughtfully planned campus, Kautilya features spacious, well-ventilated classrooms, smart digital boards, a comprehensive library, and a safe, secure environment equipped with 24x7 CCTV monitoring.

Professional Swimming Pool

The school features a well-maintained swimming pool that encourages aquatic sports and physical fitness. Swimming lessons are conducted under expert guidance with rigorous safety standards.

Athletic Playground & Sports

Physical education is a core pillar at Kautilya. The expansive sports grounds accommodate football, cricket, basketball, track & field events, fostering teamwork, discipline, and competitive spirit.

Auditorium & Cultural Spaces

Equipped with modern acoustics and lighting, the indoor auditorium hosts vibrant cultural fests (like Saamskrithika Parva), debates, annual day productions, and guest lectures.

Atal Tinkering & STEM Labs

A Niti Aayog approved Atal Tinkering Lab alongside dedicated Physics, Chemistry, and Biology laboratories where students engage in hands-on robotics, coding, and scientific research.
